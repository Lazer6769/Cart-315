I found it interesting the way he talks about the process of designing something “It is rare that the designer has the design all in her head in advance, and then merely translates it” (Schon, Bennett). He explains how someone who has the idea in their head can translate it into their own creation and think that it's perfect; it reflects on how we preserve our ideas and how those ideas can change over time. I relate to this in a way, where my original idea becomes vastly different from what I thought it could be; the execution can be from lack of experience, putting too much bias into your project making it seem that it'll be the best thing out there as well as realizing that what you thought was good can be different from your own expectations as well as how “a good designer strives to make the details work so well that they become invisible to the user.” (Schon, Bennett) it shows that we as designer care for the things that we make as well as how they will be interacted with by the user and how it will shape their experience with said material, i find that the best was to create isn't to make something perfect, I believe that we should make things that will be interesting for us to make and for the users to experience something that’ll take them out of reality and allow them to enjoy themselves something that make them want  come back and have a laugh at, something that they can come home to and actually enjoy.**
