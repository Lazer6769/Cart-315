## Intense flappy birds

For my variation I decided to focus more on the code than the visual aspect of the game so it'll look exactly like flappy bird for the sole exception of the code which has been change to allow for a more Intense game play 

### Controls 
- I left the space bar untouched since it is a vital aspect of the game 
- a thing I did add to my game was a feature I added from the first project and that is a spin feature, the bird does a full 360 before landing on its original rotation however i made it so you would get double points from spinning while in-between the pipes this gives the player the incentive to spin for 1 reason alone, style points. I also added a cooldown so that the player does not spam it constantly just so they don't go overboard with it (press the R key to spin)
- Another feature that I added was a speed boost, if the player presses the Z key they would get a slight boost of speed which has a cooldown of 2.5 seconds however one thing I did add to make it more intense was to add a overspeeding mechanic that has a chance of activating doubling the speed's boost so that the player either goes straight into the pipe or is able to succeed in going inbetween them.

#### Mechanics 

there's a couple of mechanics that I added into the game to make it more intense for the player

- I made it so that the pipes move up and down as to disorient the player as well as having the gaps between them move as well giving the player an opportunity to fly freely or to make them anxious since they would have to squeeze into the tiny gap, also at random all the pipes would stop moving for 2 seconds confusing the player since they've gotten used to the movement and they might crash after said confusion 
- another that was added was that the bird would slow down after a random roll was succeeded his vertical movement would slow down by half so you would have to be careful because that slow down can be the cost of the game 
- a funny thing I did add before it got messed up was the changing in size now I didn't do what I did last time and make the bird huge or teeny tiny, instead I just made it barely shrink and grow so that the player could not tell if it change size or not since it would also revert back to it's original size
